## Picture & Sound Performance

The Samsung S90D achieves peak HDR brightness of approximately 1,187–1,218 nits (10% window) and SDR brightness of approximately 476–505 nits, placing it among the brightest OLEDs tested and outperforming the LG C4, Sony Bravia 8, and Panasonic Z95A in SDR brightness. [S520828356706055698] [S801298564666822118]

In dark-room viewing the S90D delivers excellent contrast, deep black levels, and vibrant color coverage (99.95% UHDA-P3, 90.58% Rec. 2020), with color accuracy rated among the best tested (Delta-E of 1.26); Filmmaker mode is recommended as the most accurate picture setting. [S520828356706055698] [S801298564666822118]

In bright-room conditions the S90D's lack of the S95D's anti-glare coating results in reduced contrast and elevated black levels compared to the LG C4, which preserves contrast better with the lights on; CNET preferred the LG C4's image in bright rooms despite the S90D having slightly better reflection reduction than the ultra-glossy C4. [S801298564666822118]

## Pros, Cons & Overall Verdict

Key advantages of the S90D include exceptional off-axis viewing, high OLED brightness, deep black levels, a super-slim design with pedestal stand, strong gaming feature set, and a 40W 2.1-channel audio system with Dolby Atmos support; key drawbacks include the absence of Dolby Vision HDR, Tizen OS navigation complexity, varied panel types across sizes (QD-OLED only on 55-, 65-, and 77-inch in North America), no ATSC 3.0 tuner, and a non-backlit remote. [S520828356706055698] [S801298564666822118] [S1091192767696552021]

Critical consensus positions the S90D as one of the best-value OLED TVs available: Tom's Guide awarded it Editor's Choice calling it 'the best bang for your buck' among OLEDs; CNET scored it 8.4/10; Gadget Review assigned a True Score of 80/100. All reviewers agree it outperforms non-OLED alternatives and competes closely with the LG C4, with the primary differentiator being bright-room contrast and Dolby Vision support. [S520828356706055698] [S801298564666822118] [S1091192767696552021]

The S90D's audio system (40W, 2.1-channel, Dolby Atmos) is considered above average for a built-in TV speaker system and performs adequately in larger rooms, though reviewers note that a dedicated soundbar would provide meaningfully better audio; audio settings such as Auto Volume and Amplify are recommended for optimizing dialogue clarity and source balance. [S520828356706055698]

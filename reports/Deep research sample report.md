## Panel Technology Overview: QD-OLED vs WOLED vs WOLED Evo

The Samsung S90D (55"/65"/77" North America) uses a QD-OLED panel with an RGB triangle subpixel structure, while the LG C4 and Sony BRAVIA 8 both use WOLED panels with an RWBG subpixel layout including a white subpixel. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S855371248066094553]

QD-OLED panels like the S90D achieve color luminance approximately 140% higher than WOLED panels at peak brightness (1% APL) because the RGB-additive pixel structure allows full color power allocation, whereas WOLED's white subpixel boosts white luminance but dilutes color purity. [S905024494067075098]

Despite WOLED panels (like those in the LG C4 and Sony BRAVIA 8) reaching higher peak white luminance specs than QD-OLED, the QD-OLED panel's colors appear approximately 36% brighter in perceived brightness (XCR score) for small highlight APL, because color gamut and saturation contribute to perceived brightness beyond raw luminance. [S905024494067075098]

The WOLED white subpixel boosts peak white luminance but dilutes color purity of greens and reds, and causes 'volumetric collapse' at higher luminance where the full color range can no longer be realized—a structural disadvantage for HDR color fidelity versus QD-OLED. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S905024494067075098]

## Gaming Performance Deep-Dive

The Samsung S90F (QD-OLED) supports a 144Hz native refresh rate at 4K and has very low input lag in Game Mode, with all four HDMI ports supporting full 48Gbps HDMI 2.1 bandwidth. [S98752181701312031]

The LG C4 OLED supports a 144Hz native refresh rate at 4K with exceptionally low input lag in Game Optimizer mode (with 'Prevent Input Delay' set to 'Boost'), and all four HDMI ports support full 48Gbps HDMI 2.1 bandwidth. [S97279286438060628]

The Sony BRAVIA 8 OLED supports a maximum 120Hz refresh rate at 4K and has low input lag in Game Mode, but only HDMI ports 3 and 4 support HDMI 2.1 bandwidth (4K @ 120Hz), limiting high-bandwidth connectivity compared to rivals. [S421371176124338505]

The Samsung S90F supports all three VRR standards (HDMI Forum VRR, FreeSync, and G-SYNC Compatible) across a very wide refresh rate range with Low-Frame-Compensation (LFC) support. [S98752181701312031]

The LG C4 OLED supports FreeSync, HDMI Forum VRR, and is certified G-SYNC Compatible, ensuring a nearly tear-free gaming experience from any VRR-enabled source. [S97279286438060628]

The Sony BRAVIA 8 OLED supports HDMI Forum VRR and G-SYNC but does not support FreeSync, limiting compatibility with AMD GPU users compared to the S90F and LG C4. [S421371176124338505]

All three TVs (Samsung S90F, LG C4, Sony BRAVIA 8) have nearly instantaneous pixel response times, resulting in incredibly clear motion with almost no blur behind fast-moving objects. [S97279286438060628] [S98752181701312031] [S421371176124338505]

The Samsung S90F's pixel response at its maximum 144Hz refresh rate is fantastic with pixels transitioning almost instantly and no noticeable overshoot, delivering very sharp fast motion. [S98752181701312031]

## Audio & Design

The Samsung S90D has a 40W 2.1-channel built-in speaker system (the S90DD/S92D Canadian variant upgrades to 60W), with a decent frequency response that produces some bass and clear dialogue but does not get very loud. [S855371248066094553]

The LG C4 uses a 40W 2.2-channel speaker system, giving it one additional channel compared to the Samsung S90D's 2.1-channel system. [S609998860606067556]

The Sony Bravia 8 (XR80) uses Acoustic Surface Audio+ technology, vibrating the panel itself as the primary speaker so that dialogue and effects appear to emanate from the screen, supplemented by frame-mounted tweeters and integrated subwoofers for bass depth. [S401475683203748851] [S953802149538028370] [S1109955067113820009]

The Sony Bravia 8 supports Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, and IMAX Enhanced audio formats, and includes 3D Surround Upscaling that converts 2ch/5.1ch audio to virtual 5.1.2ch sound using the TV's own speakers. [S953802149538028370]

The Samsung S90D supports eARC with full Dolby audio passthrough (Dolby Atmos over DD+, TrueHD 7.1, LPCM 7.1) but does not support DTS formats over eARC, which is a limitation for Blu-ray users with DTS lossless audio tracks. [S855371248066094553]

Sony's Acoustic Center Sync feature allows compatible Sony soundbars (e.g., BRAVIA Theater Bar 8/Bar 9) to use the Bravia 8's Acoustic Surface panel as a center channel, creating a more cohesive front soundstage; this requires a Sony TV from 2021 or newer for calibrated operation. ## Key Trade-Offs: QD-OLED vs WOLED vs WOLED Evo

The LG C4 loses a noticeable amount of HDR brightness in Game Optimizer mode, while the Samsung S90D shows no noticeable difference in HDR brightness between its standard and Game Mode, giving the S90D a meaningful advantage for HDR gaming. [S97279286438060628] [S855371248066094553]

The Sony BRAVIA 8 shows most scenes are slightly brighter in Game Mode than in standard mode, a contrast to the LG C4 which is noticeably dimmer in Game Optimizer, making the BRAVIA 8 more consistent across modes than the C4. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505]

The Samsung S90D's QD-OLED panel lacks a polarizer, causing blacks to appear increasingly gray in bright ambient lighting, whereas the LG C4 and Sony BRAVIA 8 WOLED panels maintain exceptional black levels even in rooms with ambient lighting. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S855371248066094553]

The Samsung S90D does a great job reducing direct reflection intensity, while both the LG C4 and Sony BRAVIA 8 have alright but distracting direct reflection handling, making the S90D better suited for rooms with direct light sources opposite the screen. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S855371248066094553]

The Samsung S90D's QD-OLED triangular subpixel structure causes color fringing on horizontal lines and only okay text clarity on a PC, while the LG C4 and Sony BRAVIA 8 WOLED panels have minor ClearType issues on Windows but no color fringing. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S855371248066094553]

All three TVs have an aggressive Automatic Brightness Limiter (ABL) in HDR, making large bright scenes significantly dimmer than small specular highlights, which is a common OLED limitation rather than a differentiator between these models. [S97279286438060628] [S421371176124338505] [S855371248066094553]

The Samsung S90D QD-OLED has a higher color volume than WOLED panels because its RGB-additive structure maintains DCI-P3 coverage across its full luminance range (~116% color volume), while WOLED panels exhibit 'volumetric collapse' at higher luminance levels, dropping to ~67% DCI-P3 color volume. [S905024494067075098]

RTINGS rates the Sony BRAVIA 8 as most comparable to the LG C4 but inferior—it is not as bright in HDR, doesn't support 4k @ 144Hz, has worse out-of-box accuracy, and is more expensive, making the LG C4 the better choice for almost everyone between those two. [S421371176124338505]
